GestaltTroller is an iOS customization utility leveraging system vulnerabilities to modify MobileGestalt parameters and toggle internal feature flags without a full jailbreak.

  • Custom MobileGestalt device & UI tweaks
  • Disable or customize PosterBoard behavior
  • Toggle Liquid Glass & internal iOS Feature Flags
