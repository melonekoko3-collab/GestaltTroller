ESign / KSign Sideload Guide

  • 1Open Safari on your iPhone and install ESign or KSign via an official signing service or enterprise certificate.
  • 2Open Settings > General > VPN & Device Management and trust the enterprise certificate.
  • 3Tap Download above to get the latest GestaltTroller .ipa from GitHub Releases.
  • 4Import and sign the .ipa inside ESign/KSign using your certificate, then tap Install.

GestaltTroller is an iOS customization utility leveraging a system vulnerability to modify MobileGestalt parameters and toggle internal system feature flags without a full jailbreak.

  • Custom MobileGestalt device & UI tweaks
  • Disable or customize PosterBoard behavior
  • Toggle Liquid Glass & internal iOS Feature Flags
